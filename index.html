Content Publisher

Content Publisher is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It queues short videos and posts them to the owner's own YouTube channels and Instagram professional accounts at scheduled times, and reads back those posts' view counts so the owner can see what worked.

It only ever acts on accounts its owner signs in with. It does not post to, read from or collect data about anyone else's accounts, and it is not offered as a service to other people.

This page also hands an Instagram sign-in back to the app on the owner's computer. Nothing passing through it is stored here.
